# 🏨 Hospitality Insights | AtliQ Grand

**About the Project**  
AtliQ Grand, a chain of five-star hotels across India (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad), was facing declining revenue and market share.  
As a data consultant, I analyzed their hospitality data using **Power BI** and delivered dashboards with actionable insights to support recovery and growth.

---

Explore the live dashboard [here](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiZmMwMzJhNTAtMWJhMy00NTJkLThhMzQtMTRiMjBlYzY5NzQ3IiwidCI6ImM2ZTU0OWIzLTVmNDUtNDAzMi1hYWU5LWQ0MjQ0ZGM1YjJjNCJ9).


 
## 🎯 Goal
- Analyze hotel performance metrics  
- Track revenue across multiple dimensions  
- Understand booking behavior  
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve occupancy, revenue, and customer satisfaction  

---

## 📊 Dashboards Designed
1. **Performance Overview** → Revenue, Occupancy %, ADR, RevPAR, Satisfaction scores  
2. **Revenue Overview** → Revenue by city, category, and booking platforms  
3. **Booking Overview** → Guest booking behavior, cancellations, booking trends  

---

## 🔑 Key Insights
- Found revenue and occupancy gaps across cities  
- Analyzed platform-level contribution to bookings  
- Identified cancellation trends affecting revenue  
- Recommended strategies to recover lost market share
